    About the ministry

    ConCafe is a conversation over coffee — con café — about the texts the church reads together each week. Every episode takes a passage from the Revised Common Lectionary and asks the practical question: what does this ask of me today?

    Pastor Eradio Valverde is a United Methodist minister who has served congregations across South and Central Texas. Within the Rio Texas Annual Conference he served as District Superintendent of the Coastal Bend District until his retirement from that appointment in 2017. He began recording ConCafe in June 2019 and has published more than 1,400 episodes since.

    The episodes are short by design — most run ten to seventeen minutes, about the length of a cup of coffee. They are free to stream here, and free on every major podcast app.
